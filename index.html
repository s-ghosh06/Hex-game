Objective
Be the first to form an unbroken chain of your pieces connecting your two edges of the board. There are no draws in Hex — someone always wins (proven by John Nash, 1942).
⬡
Teal — Player 1
Your goal is to connect the Left edge → Right edge of the board. In vs AI mode you always play as Teal. In 2-Player mode, Teal goes first.
⬡
Gold — AI / Player 2
Must connect the Top edge → Bottom edge. In 2-Player mode, pass the phone to Player 2 on their turn.
👆
How to Place
Tap or click any empty hexagon to place your piece. Players alternate turns automatically. Each hex touches 6 neighbours, forming the board's unique diagonal connectivity.
🧠
Strategy
Control the centre — it connects to the most paths. Block your opponent's chain early. Every move both attacks and defends simultaneously. The Undo button removes the last 2 moves.
👥
2-Player Mode
Both players share one device. Player 1 is Teal (Left→Right), Player 2 is Gold (Top→Bottom). The screen shows whose turn it is clearly. Pass the phone after each move!

Algorithms Used
MM
Minimax Search
AI explores all moves to a set depth, alternating maximising and minimising scores. Guarantees the best move at the given depth.
Ref: Shannon (1950) — Philosophical Magazine
α-β
Alpha-Beta Pruning
Skips branches that can't affect the result. Reduces O(b^d) to O(b^(d/2)) — AI searches twice as deep in same time.
Ref: Knuth & Moore (1975) — AI Journal
UF
Union-Find (Win Check)
Detects winning connections in O(α(n)) ≈ O(1) using Disjoint Sets. Win detected when virtual edge-nodes merge.
Ref: Tarjan (1975) — Journal of the ACM
